# Ideas: landing moments, in-app delight, growth (2026-11-25)

Each idea has a rough size: **S** (a day or less), **M** (a few days), **L** (a week or more). ★ marks my top picks.

## Landing and brand moments
1. ★ **Your plot, live (M).** The hero asks one thing, "How big is your plot?", and the build film is replaced by a real 3D house growing on a plot of exactly that size, made by our own engine in the browser. It turns the landing into the first step of the questionnaire.
2. ★ **Scroll builds the house (M).** On the "How it works" scene, sideways scrolling assembles a 3D house layer by layer (plot, plinth, columns, walls, roof), using the same element library as the app. Real product, not stock footage.
3. **Day to night (S).** A slider on the designs scene moves the sun across a house; windows glow at night. Reuses the 3D viewer's sun path.
4. **Style morph (M).** Pick a style chip (Kerala, Chettinad, Contemporary) and the same house changes its roof, materials and colours in place. Shows that a style is architecture, not paint.
5. **Before / after plot slider (S).** Drag across a photo: empty red-soil plot on one side, finished house on the other (the build-film stills).
6. **Real families' homes (M, later).** Short stories of families who built from a Nestora concept, once there are some. No invented testimonials until then.

## In-app delight
7. ★ **The house grows as you answer (M).** Beside the questionnaire, a small 3D massing updates live: add a floor and it rises, add a car and a porch appears, tick "elders" and a ground-floor bedroom lights up.
8. ★ **Swipe to compare (S).** On phones, option cards stack and you swipe: right to like, left to pass, up to open. "Show more like my liked ones" becomes the natural next step.
9. **Side-by-side morph (M).** Pick two options and a slider morphs one plan into the other, rooms sliding to their new places, so the difference is obvious.
10. **Walk-in on day one (S).** After choosing a design, open straight into a 5-second guided fly-through from the gate to the living room, then hand over the controls.
11. **"What if" chips (M).** Under a design: "Add a study", "Bigger kitchen", "One more floor". Each re-solves with the new engine and shows the new plan and cost in seconds.
12. **Designing screen worth watching (S).** While options generate (now about 40 s), show the plan being solved: rooms appearing and settling, with plain-language notes ("Keeping the puja in the north-east").

## Growth
13. ★ **Share with family on WhatsApp (S).** One tap shares a preview image and link. Relatives vote with a heart on each option without signing up, and the owner sees the votes. Indian homes are joint decisions.
14. **Architect hand-off (M).** "Send to an architect" packages the plan, elevation, 3D link, brief and estimate as a PDF/DXF bundle. Later, a directory of partner architects by city (a B2B channel).
15. **Printable plan poster (S).** A nicely designed A3 PDF of the chosen home (plan, elevation, a 3D still). Something families pin up and show around.
16. **Saved homes and versions (S).** "Save" keeps every version; the owner can go back to an earlier design.
17. **City pages (M).** Plot-size landing pages ("30×40 house design in Bengaluru", with that city's rules), for search traffic and to set up the per-city rules work.
18. **Referral (S, later).** "Invite a friend who is building": both get a free premium export.

## Suggested next three
- 13 Share on WhatsApp with family voting: small effort, directly drives growth.
- 7 House grows as you answer: makes the questionnaire memorable.
- 1 or 2 on the landing: real product in 3D instead of stock imagery.
