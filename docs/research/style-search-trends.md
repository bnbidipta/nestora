## Takeaways
- Indians search generic "modern / Indian house design / villa" far more than any named style; Kerala, Chettinad,
  colonial and farmhouse are the named styles with real interest. Western named styles are near zero in India.
- Worldwide, the strong clear styles are Modern Farmhouse, Barn, Bali/Tropical, Adobe, Colonial, Mediterranean,
  English Cottage, Minimalist; Tuscan, Spanish, Bauhaus, Art Deco and Brutalist are weak.
